SAMPLE POLICY · LAST UPDATED OCTOBER 2026

1. Information we collect

A live version of Nova may collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, role, and messages sent through forms. The site may also collect limited technical information needed to operate and protect the service.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to manage early-access invitations, respond to questions, improve the product, and send updates where you have given permission. A real site should explain each purpose clearly and request consent where required by law.

3. Sharing and service providers

A real service may rely on carefully selected providers for hosting, analytics, email delivery, and support. Personal information should only be shared as necessary, under appropriate safeguards, and in accordance with applicable law.

4. Security and retention

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect personal information. Information should only be kept for as long as needed for the stated purposes or legal obligations.

5. Your choices

Users should be able to unsubscribe from marketing messages and request access, correction, or deletion of their information where applicable. A production website should provide clear instructions for these requests.

6. Contact

For this demo, contact details are placeholders. Before launch, replace them with the business’s real privacy contact and have this policy reviewed for the relevant jurisdiction.
