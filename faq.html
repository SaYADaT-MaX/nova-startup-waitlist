What is Nova? +

Nova is a concept productivity platform designed to help ambitious teams find focus, plan their work, and make progress visible.

When will Nova launch? +

We’re preparing an early-access release. Join the waitlist to receive launch updates and an invitation when access opens.

Is the waitlist free? +

Yes. Joining the waitlist is free and doesn’t commit you to purchasing a product.

Who is Nova for? +

Nova is being designed for founders, creative teams, product builders, and anyone who wants a clearer working rhythm.

How will you use my email? +

In this demo, the form only shows a local confirmation and does not send your data to a server. A live site should connect to a secure mailing-list provider and publish a complete privacy notice.

Can I share feedback before launch? +

Absolutely. Use the contact page to share your thoughts, questions, or the kind of problem you’d love Nova to solve.
